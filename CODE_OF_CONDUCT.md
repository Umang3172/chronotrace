# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the project

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments,
personal or political attacks, publishing others' private information without
permission, and other conduct which could reasonably be considered
inappropriate.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the
project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will
result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the
circumstances.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
